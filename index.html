What is this website?

This site is a small, lightweight site that is designed to give a simple overview of some of my thoughts and opinions on cinema. I designed it to give a look at a snapshot of my preferences as they stand today, but don't read too much into my choices, as things like 'my favorites' can and do change frequently. That being said, I've enjoyed film all of my life, and feel as though I've seen a fairly wide range of films from different genres, countries, time periods, etc. and wanted to give a little insight into that. This is the landing page and is relatively bereft of content, as most of the content lives in the other pages on the site.

The 'My Favorite Films' page gives a quick look at my best attempt at picking a current top five favorite films. It is the only list on the site that is ranked by my preferences, and provides the film title, director, and year of release. On this page and my watchlist page, any listed directors that are also referenced in my favorite directors page will link to that entry on that page.

The next page going over some of my favorite directors is not a ranked list, but rather is listed in rough chronological order based on when they were, or at least generally began producing works. Alongside each selection of a director (or directors in one case) is a representative snapshot of some of their films that I've seen and enjoyed, but is by no means comprehensive. Additionally those films I've included that have won the Academy Award for Best Picture have been denoted as such.

Finally I provided very short watchlist that consists of some films that I've been meaning to watch and just haven't gotten around to yet for whatever reason. They range from films considered classics that everybody should probably see at some point, to films I'm personally interested in, to something more recent that was extremely popular and well received.
